1. Why use the autoencoder at all?

Given that the AE adds irreducible distortion (1000 → 50 → 1000, mmd_ae_recon ≈ 0.08
before any transport), it's fair to ask why the pipeline keeps it.

Reasons we *currently* rely on it (not necessarily reasons it's optimal):
- IMPACT_CellOT is built on scGen's recipe: shared AE, then neural OT in latent space.
  The AE is not an optional add-on — it's where transport happens.
- OT on 1000-d sparse gene expression is expensive and high-variance; 50-d latent
  compresses the problem.
- Cross-species alignment is done in a space both species were trained to share
  (same encoder/decoder, ortholog-matched genes).
- Means mostly survive encode/decode (frac_r2_closed looks fine); the pain is
  distributional (MMD), not the average profile.

Open question (do not conclude yet): train OT directly on 1000-d (or a larger latent)
without the bottleneck? Notebook 22 §2 decoded frame suggests transport may be doing
real work once AE measurement bias is removed (~91% gap closed, m1 v08 IMPACT).
That supports fixing *how we measure*, not necessarily ripping out the AE tomorrow.

2. "Drop the AE" would follow from a naive reading of MMD — and that's the trap

First-pass story from raw metrics:
  IMPACT model MMD > identity ceiling → frac_gap_closed negative →
  "the recipe pushes the two distributions further apart than doing nothing."

That conclusion mixes reference frames:
  - imputed is decode(transport(encode(mouse))) — lives in AE-decoded space
  - mmd_floor / mmd_ceiling (raw sidecar) are on raw control / raw treated
  - the AE round-trip tax is charged to the model but not to floor/ceiling

So the AE doesn't necessarily make transport *worse* — it makes raw MMD *behave badly*
as a judge: apples-to-oranges comparisons, spurious negatives, ceiling below model
even when decoded-identity ceiling (~0.31) is far above model (~0.11).

Refined statement (what we actually learned):
  - The AE distorts distributional metrics when floor/ceiling ignore it.
  - Negative frac_gap_closed on IMPACT is often a *measurement artifact*, not proof
    OT overshot — cross-check decoded frac_gap_closed and frac_r2_closed.
  - The AE still sets a real floor on how good data-space MMD can get (can't beat
    mmd_ae_recon even with perfect latent transport).

Still open: whether the AE is worth its cost in *biology* terms, not just metric
terms — but that requires decoded (or latent-space) evaluation first, not raw MMD alone.
