## Decision

Unbalanced OT stays paused.

The Stage-0 metric code is not the explanation for the low identity score. The
paper replication is faithful, and the reconstruction metrics reproduce to
numerical precision. The VAE is not a random-number generator: it preserves
gene means and broad cell profiles while strongly shrinking cell-specific
variation. The accurate description is:

> Paper scGen is a mean-preserving denoiser but a weak paired-cell identity map,
> especially for the held-out rat-LPS6 slice.

If full-cloud fidelity is required, a bounded AE repair study is warranted
before adding an unbalanced-OT degree of freedom.

## Paper replication: both gates pass

Frozen TF1 VAEArith checkpoint:
- data: Hagai 62,114 cells × 6,619 one-to-one orthologs
- train excludes rat LPS6; rat unstimulated remains in train
- architecture: 6619-800-800-100-800-800-6619
- alpha=5e-5, dropout=0.2, lr=1e-3, 300 epochs

Two-path prediction:
- all-gene mean Pearson²: 0.906739 (paper notebook 0.910167)
- top-100 rat-LPS Wilcoxon-DEG mean Pearson²: 0.7423
  (paper notebook 0.742091)
- identity rat-unst vs rat-LPS mean Pearson²: 0.866738

The missing DEG headline was the only paper-metric gap. The paper uses 100
DEGs, not 50.

## Reconstruction audit (deterministic decode(mu(X)))

Metric axes:
- paper R²: Pearson² across gene-mean vectors
- legacy recon r²/gene: for each gene, Pearson² across paired cells; mean genes
- COD/gene: standard 1-SSE/SST across paired cells; mean genes

| Slice | MSE | paired-cell Pearson²/gene | COD/gene | mean-vector Pearson² | per-cell r |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| train subsample | 0.07685 | 0.2253 | 0.1554 | 0.9976 | 0.820 |
| rat unst (seen) | 0.04359 | 0.1235 | 0.0660 | 0.9971 | 0.891 |
| rat LPS6 (held out) | 0.04823 | 0.08618 | -0.01991 | 0.9590 | 0.877 |

Held-out calibration:
- shuffled-reconstruction Pearson²/gene: ~0.000097
- AE margin above shuffled: +0.08608
- AE MSE gain vs train-gene-mean predictor: 49.7%
- AE MSE gain vs zero predictor: 80.2%
- COD median: -0.0047; mean: -0.0199
- explained-variance mean: +0.0357
- reconstruction MMD@80: ~0.113

Therefore 0.086 is low but decisively above a pairing-null. The negative mean
COD says that, averaged equally over genes, deterministic reconstruction does
not beat each gene's own mean predictor on held-out cell-specific squared
error. Simultaneously, high mean-vector Pearson² and per-cell correlation say
the decoder preserves broad expression programs.

## What the before/after plots show

- The decoder fills zeros: held-out rat-LPS zero fraction drops from ~73.8% in
  true X to ~45.2% after round-trip.
- Positive values are shifted toward a narrower, lower-expression range.
- Per-gene variance is broadly compressed below the identity line.
- Top LPS DEGs retain ordering for some genes but their marginals and means are
  visibly attenuated.
- The held-out per-gene Pearson² distribution is concentrated near zero, while
  cell-wise correlations across genes remain high. These are different axes,
  not contradictory results.

## Code audit findings

No local metric bug found:
- vectorized per-gene Pearson² matches an explicit scipy loop
- perfect identity, shuffled-cell, gene-mean, constant-gene, affine-rescaling,
  and misordered-gene synthetic tests pass
- reconstruction values reproduce the pre-audit artifact
- decoded MMD components reproduce; the very negative fraction is driven by
  model MMD ~0.201, decoded ceiling ~0.135, floor ~0.112, hence a tiny usable
  denominator (~0.023), not an arithmetic error

Important upstream/model quirks:
1. `VAEArith.to_latent` returns a sampled z=mu+sigma*epsilon despite its name.
   Paper transport uses this sampled path; identity audit uses deterministic mu.
   Five sampled-z repeats are nearly unchanged, so sampling does not explain the
   weak identity.
2. The second decoder batch-normalization call in the vendored TF code does not
   assign its return value back to `h`. The frozen checkpoint was trained with
   this upstream behavior. Fixing it now would define a different model and
   cannot explain a local evaluation-code error.
3. Paper arithmetic uses an index array sized from mouse populations when
   indexing rat controls. Stage 0 preserves this for fidelity; changing it would
   be a new prediction recipe, not a correction to identity reconstruction.
4. Latent-frame MMD mixes sampled transport latents with deterministic-mu
   references. Treat it as a sensitivity diagnostic, not the headline.

## Bounded next study (only if approved)

Do not restart open-ended HPO. Freeze this Stage-0 dataset, split, seed, and
metric panel. Compare a small preregistered set of reconstruction models using:
- held-out COD/gene and paired-cell Pearson²
- variance retention and zero-fraction calibration
- decoded reconstruction MMD
- paper all-gene + top-100 DEG transport gates

An AE candidate must improve cell/cloud identity without losing the replicated
mean-program result. Only after that gate should transport variants resume.
